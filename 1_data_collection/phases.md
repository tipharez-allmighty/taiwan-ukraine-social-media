# Ukraine Sentiment Timeline (2022–2026)

| :--- | :--- | :--- |

| Dec 2022 – May 2023 | Hope | Endurance through the first winter of massive energy grid blackouts; anticipation of heavy Western armor arriving. |
| Jun 2023 – Dec 2023 | Fatigue | Disappointment as the summer counteroffensive stalls against heavily mined Russian defense lines. |

| Nov 2024 – Jan 2025 | Acceptance | Escalating Russian strikes on infrastructure; Kursk offensive boost fades; growing openness to diplomatic freeze. |
| Feb 2025 – Oct 2025 | Betrayal & Despair | Harsh Trump-Zelensky Oval Office clash; freezing of U.S. aid; rejection of long-range weapons; U.S. approval drops to 7% within Ukraine. |
| Nov 2025 – Aug 2026 | Self-Reliance | Acceptance of a long conflict; pivot to European financial backing and native long-range drone campaigns; over 60% prepared to endure. |
